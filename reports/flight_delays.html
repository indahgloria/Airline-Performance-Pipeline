Key findings

  • Hawaiian Airlines was the most punctual carrier (83.9% on time); PSA Airlines the least (71.1%).
  • Delays build through the day: 89% of 05:00 departures arrive on time vs 74% at 19:00. Late-arriving aircraft cause 11% of delay minutes early on and 46% at the worst hour, so one late plane pushes its later flights late too.
  • Among major airports, PBI (West Palm Beach/Palm Beach, FL) had the worst on-time departure rate (72.5%), with an average taxi-out of 17 minutes.
