Key findings

  • Hawaiian Airlines was the most punctual carrier (85.7% on time); Frontier Airlines the least (73.1%).
  • On-time rate falls from 85% for 06:00 departures to 72% by 22:00, and late-arriving aircraft go from 10% to 55% of delay minutes. Morning flights are the safest bet.
  • EWR (Newark, NJ) had the worst on-time departure rate, with 22-minute average taxi-outs pointing to ground congestion.
